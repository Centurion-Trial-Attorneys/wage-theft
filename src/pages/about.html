Why a wage theft site?

Wage theft is one of the most common workplace violations, and many workers don't know it's happening. A rounded timecard, a skipped break or a late final check can look small on one paycheck and add up to thousands of dollars over a year, across a whole crew.

This site explains California wage law in plain English and gives workers a direct line to the attorneys behind SueMyBoss.Law.
